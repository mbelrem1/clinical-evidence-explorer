    This research workspace searches PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov, supports Scopus CSV imports, and provides local screening and extraction tools.

    Research limits

    Searches retrieve source records, not verified evidence. The app does not provide semantic ranking, full-text access, automated extraction, multi-user review, or PRISMA compliance validation. Verify eligibility and findings against each source publication.
